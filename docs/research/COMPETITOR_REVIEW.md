# Comparable Indian manufacturer websites

Reviewed 21 September 2026. Seven comparable sites, selected for press work, fabrication, tooling or off-highway components. These are website observations, not factory audits or endorsements of their quality claims. No competitor image is proposed for Unit Alfa.

## 1. Deva Industries — Peenya, Bengaluru

Sources: [Home, facilities and gallery](https://www.deva.net.in/).

- **Structure:** Home, About, Services, Quality, Facility, Gallery, Contact; largely section navigation on one document.
- **Evidence:** Machinery table separates machine, make, capacity and quantity. Product gallery groups draw/forming components, assemblies, hinges and locks, with preview links. A certificate download is offered.
- **Visuals:** Pale blue/white, blue accents, machine cutouts in the hero, serif wordmark. Browser-computed body type is Inter, so the typography is not the proposed model for Unit Alfa.
- **Enquiry:** Named contacts and roles, email, two factory addresses and map link. No drawing-upload form observed on the reviewed page.
- **Follow:** Machine table and named product categories. **Avoid:** Generic “Get Started” CTA and borrowing claims or logos. Unit Alfa should use “Request a quote.”

## 2. MST Precision Technology — Hosur

Sources: [Home](https://mstprecision.in/), [Infrastructure](https://mstprecision.in/infrastructure/), [Gallery](https://mstprecision.in/gallery/), [Contact](https://mstprecision.in/contact-us/).

- **Structure:** Home, About, Infrastructure, Gallery, Contact.
- **Evidence:** Large factory-interior banner; infrastructure lists named machines, capacities and makes, including machining and forming equipment. Gallery is separate from corporate narrative.
- **Visuals:** White header, restrained red active navigation, darkened plant photograph; Open Sans body and Roboto headings in computed styles.
- **Enquiry:** Direct Contact CTA. Contact page retrieval was intermittent, so detailed form fields/upload behavior were not established.
- **Follow:** Actual shop-floor context and a compact equipment reference. **Avoid:** Long mission/vision copy and unqualified superlatives. Keep Unit Alfa's key capacities legible without traversing a slide show.

## 3. TSK Industrial Services — Hosur

Sources: [Home](https://www.tskindustrialservices.com/), [Contact](https://www.tskindustrialservices.com/contact-us).

- **Structure:** About, multiple service pages, product branches, Gallery and Contact. Press components sit alongside packaging and consumer-oriented products.
- **Evidence:** Process text includes press tonnage, thickness and materials. Gallery has Press & Welding as a category.
- **Visuals:** Yellow/black logo, dark navy footer, yellow accents and white surfaces; Poppins headings and Open Sans body. Public page loaded after an automatic browser check.
- **Enquiry:** Name, phone, email and message; persistent WhatsApp shortcut; industrial address prominent. No drawing-upload field observed.
- **Follow:** Address visibility and an obvious mobile contact route. **Avoid:** “Coffee Cups” counters, generic industry slogans and a broad unrelated navigation tree. Do not import its packaging business into Unit Alfa's identity.

## 4. Excellent Dies & Moulds — Bhosari, Pune

Sources: [Home/products](https://www.excellentdies.com/), [Tool room](https://www.excellentdies.com/tool-room.php), [Enquiry/contact](https://www.excellentdies.com/contact.php).

- **Structure:** About, Automobile/Non-Automobile products, engineering services, Tool Room, Clients, Contact, downloadable brochure.
- **Evidence:** Finished parts are captioned with material grade, thickness or dimensions. Separate tooling and inspection services establish technical context.
- **Visuals:** Red, charcoal and white; prominent brochure button; Figtree body and Red Rose headings in computed styles. Intro loader and dense navigation create avoidable delay/clutter.
- **Enquiry:** Company, phone, email, product, city, message and upload; address plus map and direct contacts.
- **Follow:** Part-specific technical captions and product-aware RFQ with attachment support. **Avoid:** Copying customer names, or contradictory origin dates: home has a 1985 label and a 1999 founding timeline. Unit Alfa needs one evidence-backed 1991 reference.

## 5. Sri Sai Industries — Peenya, Bengaluru

Sources: [Home](https://srisaiindustries.com/), [Capabilities](https://srisaiindustries.com/capabilities/), [Contact](https://srisaiindustries.com/contact/).

- **Structure:** Home, Capabilities, Process, About, Contact. Capabilities includes product examples and expandable equipment groups.
- **Evidence:** Plant imagery, machine tables and staged treatment sequence. Actual capability claims are published alongside capacity values.
- **Visuals:** Paper-like background, faint grid, orange CTA, black condensed Big Shoulders Display headings, Karla body; blue appears as a secondary badge. Strong typography and photography dominate.
- **Enquiry:** Requests drawings/specifications or sample; phone/email/factory visit, hours, map and a list of useful RFQ details.
- **Follow:** Process-linked machine data and plain enquiry instructions. **Avoid:** Closely copying its hero composition or transplanting tolerances, in-house coating claims and customer logos. Unit Alfa's photography limits demand its own proportions.

## 6. Orbit Laser Cutting — SIPCOT-1, Hosur

Sources: [Home](https://orbitlasercutting.com/), [Request a quotation](https://orbitlasercutting.com/contact).

- **Structure:** About, process/service pages, Projects, Gallery, Contact; brochure access repeated.
- **Evidence:** Machine image, named equipment/capacities, product images identified as brochure-derived. One process is explicitly identified as an associate-partner facility.
- **Visuals:** Charcoal, white and muted teal, image-backed hero and rectangular CTAs. Computed type is Inter; do not adopt it as Unit Alfa's identity.
- **Enquiry:** Clear drawing/material/thickness/quantity request; form includes name, company, email, phone, service and requirement. Working hours, factory contacts and map are visible. No upload field was present in the inspected form.
- **Follow:** Distinguish owned equipment from partner access; explain what to send for a useful quote. **Avoid:** Adopting its response-time promise or implying Unit Alfa has the same coating/laser infrastructure.

## 7. NASH Industries — Bengaluru / off-highway benchmark

Sources: [Home](https://www.nashindia.com/), [Stamping](https://www.nashindia.com/stamping.html), [Fabrication](https://www.nashindia.com/light-and-heavy-fabrication.html), [Welding lines](https://www.nashindia.com/welding-lines.html), [Off-highway](https://www.nashindia.com/off-highway.html), [Contact](https://www.nashindia.com/contact.html).

- **Structure:** Capabilities grouped under Design, Manufacture and Assemble; industry pages include Off-Highway; separate Quality and Contact.
- **Evidence:** Named press and weld-line photography; off-highway fuel/oil tanks and subassemblies shown on simple backgrounds.
- **Visuals:** White, strong corporate blue, restrained grey headings/rules, large finished-part imagery and sidebar categories. Computed font is a custom family called `callun-regular`.
- **Enquiry:** Sales/general email routes, map and conventional name/email/phone/subject/message form. Legal company identifier is visible in the footer.
- **Follow:** Process and application are different navigation concepts; parts lead industry pages. **Avoid:** Corporate-scale menu complexity and inferring equivalent production scale for Unit Alfa.

## Patterns to carry forward

| OEM buyer question              | Useful observed pattern                              | Unit Alfa response                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Can this shop make my part?     | Captioned products plus process/equipment references | Exact product names, source photographs and drawing-specific enquiries.                            |
| Is the capacity suitable?       | Machine/make/capacity tables                         | Publish known ratings and clearly distinguish unknowns.                                            |
| What evidence supports quality? | Certificate download or quality page                 | Historical ISO reference only until current certificate is supplied.                               |
| How do I get a quote?           | Drawings/sample request; product/contact form        | Part, quantity, material, thickness, industry and drawing availability; genuine delivery feedback. |
| Where is the factory?           | Address, industrial estate, map, direct contact      | Consistent No. 15 address, GSTIN and entrance photo; verified pin.                                 |
| Can I use this on my phone?     | Persistent direct contact                            | Compact sticky RFQ button with clear focus; no overlapping call/WhatsApp/chat bubbles.             |

GSTIN was not uniformly visible across the reviewed comparator pages; address and contact information were much more consistent. Adding Unit Alfa's verified GSTIN is a sensible trust aid, not an asserted industry-wide pattern. Public engineering drawings were not a strong shared pattern: finished products and equipment were. Drawings belong in a private enquiry flow unless an example has been cleared for publication.

Trust comes from specific, internally consistent evidence. Avoid counters without context, generic certifications, unrelated stock factories, decorative loaders and vague “solutions” copy. None of the sampled forms was submitted or tested for actual delivery.
