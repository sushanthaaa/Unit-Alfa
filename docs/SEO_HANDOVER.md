# Search and answer-engine handover

The site makes the company and its actual manufacturing scope clear in crawlable HTML: Bommasandra/Bengaluru location, press components, fabrication and assemblies, excavator applications, materials, tooling and contact details. Product pages explain the part and the information a buyer should supply. This supports both conventional search and systems that extract answers from public pages.

## Implemented

- Distinct titles, descriptions and headings; canonical URLs and Open Graph metadata.
- One Organization/LocalBusiness identity with verified address, phone, email and GSTIN; Product manufacturer references and breadcrumb data on detail pages.
- Seven substantive product detail pages linked from the catalogue, capability and enquiry pages.
- XML sitemap, robots policy, clean URLs, true 404 handling and redirects for known legacy URLs including Quality.
- Static HTML, self-hosted fonts, responsive WebP photographs, image dimensions, lazy loading and small progressive-enhancement modules.
- Review deployments, staff routes, private tracking routes and APIs stay out of search. Unknown dimensions, planned facilities, invented offers and unverifiable certifications are excluded.

## After approval and domain cutover

1. Verify the canonical company domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submit `/sitemap-index.xml`.
2. Update the existing Google Business Profile and legitimate directory records using the approved company name, address, phone, email and domain consistently. Do not create duplicate local listings.
3. Confirm old indexed URLs and any backlinks, adding specific redirects where needed. Keep product/capability links descriptive and stable.
4. Monitor query impressions, indexing, mobile field performance and actual enquiry quality. Use real questions from procurement discussions to improve visible page copy.
5. Add confirmed certificate documents, production photographs and new capabilities only when publication is approved. Keep dates and facts current.

No framework, schema or `llms.txt` file guarantees first position in search or inclusion in an LLM answer. This implementation does not claim rankings, rich-result eligibility or measured Core Web Vitals. Product pages have no fictitious price, review or availability fields. Avoid hidden keyword blocks, repetitive city pages and unsupported superlatives.

Staff/customer private records must never be added to the sitemap, public structured data, analytics payloads or AI discovery files.
